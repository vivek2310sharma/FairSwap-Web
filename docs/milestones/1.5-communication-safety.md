# Milestone 1.5 - communication, reporting, support, and privacy

## Gate and outcome

Reference contract only. Requires accepted 1.4 and a reviewed 1.5 plan before BUILD. Complete the human communication/safety workflow with real local services; no claim of public launch readiness until 1.6.

## Plan

Design immutable conversation participants, message idempotency/read state, RLS/Realtime authorization, block semantics, notification outbox, durable quotas, reports/support/evidence access, privacy processing and audit. Define what continues after a listing is sold/removed or an account is suspended/deleted. Specify staff evidence access without blanket private-message browsing.

## Build batches

A. Messaging: one unique buyer/listing conversation with trusted seller, persisted safe text, stable pagination, unread/read markers, sender-scoped request IDs, failure/retry and reconnect recovery. No self-chat, participant substitution or client-assigned sender. Existing authorized history follows the documented lifecycle policy; new enquiries require available content.

B. Live updates and notifications: RLS-protected Postgres Changes for initial delivery; explicitly authorize any additional channel features. In-app notifications and preferences are real. Optional activity email is enabled only with real configuration and truthful delivery records. No successful-send label before storage/required processing succeeds.

C. Blocks and abuse controls: enforce both-direction blocks for new messages, current restrictions even with old sessions, and meaningful database/server-backed quotas wherever direct API writes exist. Client-only buttons and route-only rate limits are insufficient.

D. Reports and support: report listing/account/message with legitimate target visibility; track assignment, status, reason and resolution; hide reporter identity. Implement tickets/replies with internal notes separate from user-visible replies, moderation appeals, and restricted case-scoped evidence access. Audit sensitive access/actions.

E. Privacy: complete real data-access/deletion-request handling started in 1.2, with requester verification, explicit retention/anonymization behavior, user-visible status, and narrowly scoped privileged Auth cleanup. Do not cascade away another participant's justified history. Implement authenticated idempotent processing/retries and a documented staff fallback for failed jobs. Policy text stays draft until reviewed by the business/legal owner.

Integrate user controls with the now-real public listing/contact/report and account-support routes. Seed only synthetic local conversations/reports/tickets. Do not send real communications or introduce attachments during this milestone.

## Tests

Use separate actual browser/Auth sessions for buyer and seller. Verify persistence/live updates/reconnection; outsider data and subscription denial; fake participant/sender changes; duplicate retry idempotency; unread correctness; self-chat denial; block in either direction; suspension with existing sessions; no new enquiry on unavailable listing; notification ownership; abuse quotas through direct API; reporter privacy; support-role boundaries; case-only evidence; MFA requirements; append-only audit; safe deletion/retry and retained shared history.

Test actual failures and recovery rather than returning mock delivery success. Apply incremental migrations to a local disposable instance and test upgrade. Run full relevant previous regression checks.

## Exclusions

No photo/voice/video messages, paid goods, escrow, delivery, dispute refunds, structured offers, AI moderation-only decision maker, or verified-purchase reviews.

## Acceptance

- Buyer and seller communicate reliably with protected persistent history.
- Live events, notifications, blocks and restrictions behave correctly under real sessions.
- Reports reach authorized staff; appeals/support protect private evidence and reporter identity.
- Privacy requests complete through a tested, documented, least-privilege process.
- Database/Realtime/browser and failure/abuse tests pass; prior functionality still works.
- Operational/privacy limitations and legal/provider blockers recorded; stop before 1.6.
